## Skill recipe text (for `skills/motion-pack/SKILL.md`, the merge owner pastes it)

Decision tree, under "Story with a turn": `├─ Film trailer, teaser, title sequence or a short film from footage ──> RECIPE T (film)`.

```
### RECIPE T: film trailer, title sequence, short film, templates `templates/trailer`, `templates/title-sequence`, `templates/short-film`

1. **Pick the genre** (`layers/presets/film-looks.js` FILM_GENRES: thriller, scifi, horror, drama, documentary, noir, period, anime-ink). It sets the look (`look.film-<genre>`, a generated .cube and the SVG filter the page uses), halation, gate weave, grain, dust, the letterbox (2.39 / 2.0 / 1.85 on 16:9; 9:16 is re-laid out, never letterboxed), the title style and the trailer score.
2. **Trailer** (`node make.mjs [--set length=30|15] [--set aspect=9:16]`): cold open -> your production card (never first) -> the world -> the inciting beat -> act 2 (the cut rate halves, cards on the braam downbeats) -> act 3 (every bar a cut and a braam, 8th notes at the end) -> the silence before the hit (1-3 s, plan.audio.quiet gap) -> the title ON the hit -> a button -> the billing block -> the release card. The score (`music/trailer_styles.py`) is rendered for the exact cut; the cut-downs come from the same plan. Never double the score's braams and title hit with sfx.
3. **Title sequence** (30-60 s): one name at a time on a beat, over textures or footage; the title late; "directed by" last.
4. **Short film**: scenes of shots (your footage, the three.js stage render, or labelled plates), chapter cards, typed slugs, subtitles from a lines or words JSON (no generated voice), the J / L builder for each scene's sound, the score entering on emotion, a dip into the credits roll.
5. **Honesty:** no studio logos, rating marks or real titles; `card.quote` needs a source (blank = a red card); "from the makers of" and the date card print placeholder labels until they are real; the film pill "placeholder footage · fictional film" stays on with placeholder footage.
6. **Check:** `node <template>.test.mjs`, then render, `templates/_shared/sheet.py`, `eyes/look.py --plan out/plan-<tag>.json` (the plan asks for the letterbox in `plan.eyes`), `ears/listen.py --plan ...` (role trailer).
```

Reach-for-X rows: `| a film trailer, its 30 / 15 s cut-downs | templates/trailer, music/trailer_styles.py, bundle trailer, cuts/trailer_plan.py |`, `| a trailer title card | title.trailerSlab / Thin / Distressed / Glow / Cut |`, `| a billing block, end credits | credits.billingBlock (synthetic condensed), credits.roll |`, `| a film look on footage | layers/film.js mountFilmFinish(el, genre): grade + halation + weave + flicker on the picture wrapper |`, `| a film burn or an anamorphic flare | layers/filmBurn (peaks ON the cut), layers/anamorphicFlare |`.

## Integration (xp-integrate, 16 Oct): beats on the beat sheets

The three film plans now carry `plan.structure` (their own beats named with the pattern's section names), so `tools/structure_check.py` scores them. Scores at the default cut (16:9; the trailer's committed 9:16 plan scores the same):

| template | pattern | before | after | how the beats map |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| trailer | trailer | 66 | **94** | cold -> coldopen; production card + world + inciting beat -> act1; m2 -> act2; m3 -> act3; the planned silence + the title hit -> title (the pattern lists `gap` under title); button; billing block + release card -> date |
| title-sequence | title-sequence | 70 | **83** | texture + the "presents" card -> motif; the names -> credits; the main title and its "directed by" card -> maintitle |
| short-film | short-film | 44 | **78** | each demo shot names its `beat` in CONFIG (lamp coldopen, radio want, window + coast obstacle, stage turn, ship consequence, the last radio shot resolution); a chapter card takes the beat of the shot it opens; the credits are the tag. The scene slugs now hold 2.2 s after they type on (they read at 18 chars/s before) |

Where the pattern does not fit, kept as is (not mapped to null, not stretched to game the number):
- **trailer:** the demo is front-loaded: the cold open + world take 40% (the pattern's act 1 ends at 30%), so the title lands at 78% instead of 88-94%. A real cut with a longer act 2 / 3 moves it.
- **title-sequence:** the pattern ends on the main title; this template puts "directed by" last on purpose, so it is counted inside the title block. Pace scores 0.2 because the textures hold 9 s under the names (the names change every 3 s, but the checker counts texture shots). At `length=60` the demo's 10 names fill only 34 s and the rest is a hold, so the 60 s cut scores 66: per-name time does not stretch to the length. Open follow-up: stretch `per` to fill the length, or add names.
- **short-film:** pace scores 0: the plan's `audio.mode` is `continuous` (ears scores motion events against onsets), so structure_check reads motion events against a 3.5 s ceiling, and the demo holds 4-6 s plates with nothing landing. `plan.audio.events` now lists every real event (cuts, title, slugs, chapter card, subtitles, credits roll), and gaps of 4.4-5.5 s remain. A structure needs a beat on every story shot; without beats, plan.structure is omitted and the checker says so.
