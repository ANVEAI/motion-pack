# Reference study, round 3: 25 launch and motion films (14 Oct 2026)

There are 25 studies here: the 21 requested, plus hyperframes, mutchtaba2, fishaudio-2 and hanifproduktif. All were measured with `tools/refstudy.py`, and every sheet and timeline was looked at.

**Field key:**
- **bm%:** bookmarks ÷ views.
- **v/f:** views ÷ followers.
- **sync:** the share of cuts within 2 frames of an onset.
- **Tempo** is quoted only when confidence is ≥ 0.5.

**How to use these:** they teach craft only. Never copy a brand, footage, wording or an exact sequence.

**Use:** craft reference only. Stills, transcripts and measurements stay outside the repository (`reference-intake.md`); this page is a paraphrased study. The patterns that recur across the batch, with evidence tiers, are in `skills/motion-pack/references/success-patterns.md` ("Round 3").

## austinbauwens: science explainer (plastic can't be recycled forever)
**Source:** https://x.com/austinbauwens/status/2110005126396334099
**Reception:** 3.9K views (0.41× followers), 219 likes, **122 bm (3.1%)**, 5 replies.
**Format:** 20 s, 16:9.

| t | beat | treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | claim | a bottle; the cap pops; small words placed in space, timed to the VO |
| 3.5–9 | mechanism | a continuous zoom **into** the material, showing a chain of links |
| 9–13 | heat | the colour temperature shifts from blue to orange, and the links snap |
| 13–17 | consequence | the bottle crumbles to shards |
| 17–20 | title | a giant, cropped two-line title in front of the object |

**Sound:**
- VO at 163 wpm.
- −22.5 LUFS (too quiet), bass 22%.
- One cut, on an onset.

**Learn:**
- A powers-of-ten dive from object to micro-structure.
- Colour temperature as the narrative variable.
- VO-synced words that sit beside the object rather than as captions.
