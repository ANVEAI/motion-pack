# Launch films that worked (and two that didn't), 2023–2026

Evidence behind `skills/motion-pack/references/launch-playbook.md`. <!--n:launch_films-->20<!--/n--> launch films, compiled 14 Oct 2026.

**How the numbers were gathered:**
- Engagement figures were read that day from public post metadata (the fxtwitter API for X, YouTube page metadata).
- Structure comes from frame sheets and ffmpeg scene detection on low-resolution copies, kept only for analysis and never redistributed.
- Cuts per minute are a floor: scene detection misses dissolves and in-shot motion.
- **INFERRED** marks our reading, not a sourced fact.
- Music tempo could not be measured reliably and is not reported.

**Read reception carefully.** Views alone mislead: several films with millions of views have like rates near 0.04–0.1%, a pattern consistent with paid impressions (INFERRED from the rate; nothing here shows that any reach was paid for). The useful signals are like, bookmark and quote rates, and a disclosed outcome (orders, waitlist, raise).

**What the "shape" and "what to take" columns are.** Our reading of what each film did and what a maker could borrow. They are observations plus craft opinion, never findings about why a named film did or did not travel; where a cause is cited, a source is linked.

Criticism to learn from:
- Ghost's speed claims were questioned.
- Hark opened with "done with computers" and then demoed on a computer ([Gadget Flow](https://thegadgetflow.com/blog/first-impressions-hark-pro/)).
- Argon drew "benchmaxxing" doubts.
- Oki's price changed after the film.

**Production case: Lago** ([post](https://getlago.com/blog/we-killed-our-motion-design-job)):
- An agent and a skill write a typed `storyboard.json`.
- A grey-box animatic is checked on mute before the final render.
- The UI comes from a closed palette of real Figma frames: the agent "cannot invent UI".
- Two human gates; the master is cut to 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16.
