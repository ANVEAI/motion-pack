# Brewly: before you run out · script v1

ad (launch teaser) · 15 s · 30 fps · 9:16 lead · music-led, sound effects, no voice-over

Brewly is a **fictional** coffee-subscription app made for this demo. Nothing in this film is a real product, screen, number or customer.

## The four lines
- **Logline:** A half-awake home brewer shakes the last beans out of the bag on a workday morning, and her phone lights up to say the next bag is already at the door.
- **Stake:** an empty coffee bag and an empty mug at 7:02 on a workday morning.
- **Turn:** the phone on the counter lights up: the next bag is already at the door (5.0 s, 33%).
- **Payoff:** a full, steaming mug beside the Brewly name: the empty mug from the hook, filled.
- **Sound-off sentence:** After one watch with the sound off, a viewer can say: "She ran out of coffee, and Brewly had already sent the next bag."
- **Send line (social cuts):** "send this to the friend who always runs out of coffee"

## Characters and world
- **The brewer** is never seen whole: a pair of hands and a morning. The film stays on the objects she touches, so the viewer reads themselves into her.
- **The one location:** a kitchen counter at dawn, drawn flat and warm (paper, kraft, espresso brown).
- **Props as the story:** the kraft coffee bag (empty, then fresh on the end card), the white mug (empty in the hook, full and steaming on the end card), the phone (dark, then lit by Brewly).
- **Light as the state:** the counter is dim and cool while the bag is empty; the phone's screen glow warms the frame at the turn; the end card is the warmest frame.

## Scenes
1. **s01 (0-2.5 s).** An upside-down kraft coffee bag shakes over an empty white mug. Three last beans tumble into the mug, tick, tick, tick, then the bag shakes empty. A punchy hit on frame 0 and a dry paper shake. On screen: "Out of beans. Again."
2. **s02 (2.5-3.75 s).** Hard cut to a dark card: the kitchen clock, "7:02 AM." A card pop and one clock tick.
3. **s03 (3.75-5.0 s).** The empty mug alone on the counter, no steam; the phone lies dark beside it. A hollow ceramic clink. On screen: "Empty mug."
4. **s04 (5.0-7.2 s).** A riser ends on a whip into the phone as it lights up and buzzes: a Brewly notification reads "Next bag: at your door." The Brewly word mark lands above it on a cinema hit and holds. Label: [Fictional app · concept].
5. **s05 (7.2-9.0 s).** A warm card: "Coffee that *never runs out*." A card pop.
6. **s06 (9.0-12.5 s).** Push into the Brewly app screen. A bean gauge drains toward empty with soft ticks; at the low mark it flips to "Next bag shipped" with a check and a success chime. On screen: "Ships your next bag before you run out." Label: [Fictional app · demo screen].
7. **s07 (12.5-15.0 s).** An iris opens on the end card: a full, steaming mug beside "Brewly." and a "Coming soon" pill, `brewly.example` under it, fine print [Fictional brand · concept teaser]. A two-note sonic sting; the last 0.15 s is silent.
