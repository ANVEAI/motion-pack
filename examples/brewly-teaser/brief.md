# Brief: Brewly launch teaser

- **Who it is for:** people who brew coffee at home every morning and keep running out of beans; app-curious, scrolling X and Reels on the way to work.
- **Objective:** awareness: remember the name "Brewly" and the one idea that it sends the next bag before you run out.
- **Platform and lead aspect:** X (native video) and Reels/Shorts, **9:16 lead** (1080x1920). A 16:9 re-layout is optional, only if the time box allows.
- **Length:** 15 s (no cut-downs in this run).
- **The one thing to remember:** your next bag of coffee is already on its way before the last one runs out.
- **Proof we actually have:** none. Brewly is a **fictional** brand made for this demo: no users, no numbers, no reviews, no real screens. Every screen is a designed illustration.
- **Product status:** fictional (concept only). Labels: "Fictional app · concept" on product shots, "Fictional brand · concept teaser" on the end card.
- **Must say / must not say:** must show the name "Brewly" by about 5 s and on the end card. Must not say: prices, delivery times, "free", "#1", "best", "guaranteed", any percentage, any real coffee or delivery brand.
- **Tone:** warm, crisp morning energy; a small wry joke in the hook, confident at the reveal.
- **Sound:** music-led with sound effects, no voice-over; on-screen text in English only.
- **References (craft only):** none supplied.
- **Assumptions I made:** (1) the audience is home brewers, not cafe-goers; (2) the one feature is "smart refill" (the app ships the next bag before you run out), chosen because it carries the sharpest everyday stake (an empty bag on a workday morning); (3) the CTA is "Coming soon" because a fictional app cannot honestly ask for a download; (4) the URL is `brewly.example` (a reserved, non-resolving domain); (5) X is the first platform because the user asked for an X post.
