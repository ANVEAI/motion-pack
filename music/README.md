# motion-pack / music : procedural music kit + the mixer helper

Everything is generated by code from `sfx/mpaudio` voices (owned synthesis: no samples, no downloads). 65 items: 41 beds, 10 stingers, 14 sonic logos; tempo snapped so one beat is a whole number of frames; a mixer that places hits one frame after the visuals, 3-6 dB over the bed, and masters to -14 LUFS-I / -1.5 dBTP.

## Build and QA
```bash
cd music
python3 build_music.py [--fps 30]       # library/{beds,stingers,logos}/*.wav, per-bed .json sidecars (events in frames), manifest.json, tempo_maps.json  (about 45 s)
python3 qa.py                           # loudness, true peak, determinism, loop seam (step + spectral flux), beat events on whole frames, key chroma, bass share, hook, trailer markers / gap / crescendo / hold, python-vs-ffmpeg
python3 tests/test_music.py             # Euclid, chords in key, deterministic compose + grid, hook in first 3 frames, seamless loop, duck depth/look-ahead, cue lint
python3 tests/test_expansion.py         # stereo loop tiling fix, just intonation, tempo ramp on whole frames, thekas seamless, new presets deterministic, sitar logo, every transition kind sweetened
python3 tests/test_trailer.py           # trailer arc: markers on whole frames at 24/25/30/60 fps, deterministic, length rule, gap / crescendo / hit / quiet hold / digital-zero tail, ears finds the gap and the crescendo at the marker times
python3 demo/demo_expansion.py          # 24 s India-market spot: looped indian_fusion bed + new SFX families + formerly pending sweeteners + sitar logo -> demo/out/expansion/master.wav
python3 demo/demo_mix.py                # 21 s launch-ad track: bed + word-free voice stand-in + 11 transition sweeteners + UI cues + riser into logo -> demo/out/master.wav
python3 mixer.py cuesheet.json --out DIR   # CLI mixer for any cue sheet
```

## Components
| module | what | key API |
|---|---|---|
| `grid.py` | tempo snap (`bpm_snap = fps*60/round(fps*60/bpm)`), beat/bar frames, cut maps, `tempo_maps.json` | `snap_bpm`, `tempo_map`, `cut_map(bpm, fps, bars, pattern)`; patterns steady_beat, bar_hold, two_beat, half_beat, accelerate, drop_build, syncopated |
| `presets.py` | 10 genre/mood presets, 10 progressions, structure templates (loop_8, ad_30s = 15 bars exactly 30.0 s at 120 BPM, reel_15s, cine_30s/60s, hook_6s), stem balance trims | `PRESETS`, `STRUCTURES`, `TRIMS_DB` |
| `compose.py` | deterministic composer: Euclidean drums, kick-locked bass, voice-led triads, motif + Markov variation lead, energy ladder, pre-drop gap, riser + crash + impact at drops, optional kick sidechain pump, seamless loops (ring-out folded onto the head), stems drums/bass/harmony/lead/fx | `compose(preset, structure, seed, fps, bpm=None, key=None)` -> `{stems, mix, events (frames), meta}` |
| `theka.py` | tabla thekas (teental 16, keherwa 8, dadra 6 matras) over a tanpura, and a 12/8 bhangra dhol chaal; every matra on whole frames; seamless; sidecars carry sam / khali frames | `render_theka(name, fps, cycles, seed)`, `render_bhangra(fps, bars, seed)` |
| `logo.py` | sonic logos; `motif_from_name(name)` SHA-256 -> stable original motif (pattern, notes, rhythm, hold) | `render_logo(name=...)`, `render_logo(pattern=...)` |
| `stingers.py` | 10 musical stingers in A (buttons, rise-hits, resolves, tension, bounce, lofi, epic swell, outro) | `STINGERS` |
| `mixer.py` | `Library`, `Mix` (bed / vo / cues / transitions), over-bed solver, duck + carve, tier-1 pre-hit vacuum, tier-2 make-room dip, true-peak limiter, master, AAC probes, phone check, stems, `lint_cues`, CLI | `Mix.add_bed/add_vo/add_cue/add_transition/render` |

### Cue sheet (JSON) for `mixer.py`
```json
{"fps": 30, "length_s": 20, "bed": {"file": "library/beds/bed_tech_pulse_loop8.wav", "loop": true},
 "vo": {"file": "vo.wav", "start_frame": 45},
 "cues": [{"id": "snd.hit.thud", "frame": 120, "tier": 2, "over_bed_db": 4.5}, {"id": "snd.ui.pop", "frame": 140, "tier": 3, "over_vo_db": -14}],
 "transitions": [{"kind": "whip", "frame": 90, "duration_frames": 8, "energy": 0.6}],
 "master": {"lufs": -14, "ceiling_db": -1.5}}
```
`frame` = the VISUAL frame. The mixer places the anchor at (frame + 1) x SR/fps; risers end one frame earlier. `over_bed_db` is K-weighted (60 ms from the anchor, 200 ms before it for risers) against the un-dipped bed; `over_vo_db` references the voice instead (talking-head rule: tier-3 cues -12..-16 dB under the voice). If the limiter budget (<= 1.5 dB tier 1-2, <= 1.0 dB tier 3) cannot be met, the OFFENDING cue is lowered and the report shows target vs measured over-bed; a dense bed can make a +4.5 dB hit unreachable: lower the bed (`gain_db`) or accept.

### House rules enforced or linted (docs/research/sound.md section 3)
R1 one frame after the picture (exact, tested) | R2 over-bed table (hero +4.5, whoosh +2, ui +4, logo +5; > +6 flagged) | R4 hierarchy (tier-1 <= one per ~5 s and never within 3 s; tier-2 average spacing >= 1.0 s) | R5 duck 9 dB, look-ahead 80 ms, attack 15 / hold 120 / release 350 ms, mid carve | R6 pre-hit vacuum | R9 round-robin seeds for repeated ids | R10 short-form needs an audible event on frames 0-3 | R12 phone check (+ lint for sub-only cues) | stems sum exactly to the pre-limiter master.

## Trailer arc (2026-10-14, gap `music/bed_trailer_arc`): a bed with a planned valley, not a loop
`trailer.py` (`render_trailer(name, fps, seed, act_bars)`, preset `TRAILERS` in `presets.py`) renders the shape the round 4/5 trailer references share: **act 1** low drone + sub pulse (a softer second pulse a dotted 8th later, dark strings swelling in the last two bars) -> **act 2** rising 16th ostinato (roots climb i, III, iv, V; the filter opens; taiko enters halfway) -> **act 3** the full wall (taiko ostinato, sub kicks, snares, 16th ticks, tremolo strings in two octaves, brass stabs and a brass line; a crash, an impact and a one-bar noise riser into it) -> **the planned gap** (the wall stops dead on a 12 ms fade; only a sustained A1 sub remains, set 38 dB under the wall) -> **a 2-bar crescendo** (VI -> V; snare roll in 8ths, 16ths, then 32nds; taiko and strings swelling about 20 dB; a riser) -> **the title hit** (taiko, sub kick, crash, brass and low strings; every other layer ends ON the hit sample) -> **a quiet hold** (the hit rings out, then near-silence; the last 0.15 s is digital zero). Lyria (`cloud/`) cannot place markers; this bed can.

| marker | frame (30 fps) | s | what |
|---|---|---|---|
| `act1_start` | 0 | 0.00 | act 1: low drone + sub pulse |
| `act2_start` | 240 | 8.00 | act 2: rising 16th ostinato |
| `act3_start` | 480 | 16.00 | act 3: the full wall (crash + impact here) |
| `gap_start` | 720 | 24.00 | the wall stops dead; sub-only valley begins (mark it `kind: gap` in `plan.audio.quiet`) |
| `gap_end` | 810 | 27.00 | valley ends (= crescendo_start) |
| `crescendo_start` | 810 | 27.00 | crescendo begins (an audible onset) |
| `visual_cut_frame` | 929 | 30.97 | cut the picture to black here: one frame BEFORE the hit (rule R1) |
| `hit` | 930 | 31.00 | title hit and hard stop; its attack sample is hit x SR / fps |
| `quiet_hold_start` | 975 | 32.50 | the hit has rung out; near-silence (mark it `kind: hold`) |
| `end` | 1080 | 36.00 | last frame (exclusive); the last 0.15 s is digital zero |

- **Not a loop; the length rule.** Fixed length (36 s at the default 4 + 4 + 4 act bars). Never loop or crossfade it into itself. To fit a cut, re-render with `render_trailer(act_bars=(a1, a2, a3))` (whole bars, acts 1 to 3 only, all before the gap), or trim the WAV by whole bars inside acts 1 and 2 only. The gap, crescendo, hit and hold keep fixed beat lengths. The sidecar (`bed_trailer_arc.json`) carries every marker in frames and seconds, `bar_frames`, `beat_frames`, `kick_frames`, `riser_frames` and the `length_rule`.
- **Plan it for ears.** The gap and the hold are intended shape: put them in the composition plan as `plan.audio.quiet` `[{"t": [24.0, 27.0], "kind": "gap", "why": "..."}, {"t": [32.5, 36.0], "kind": "hold", "why": "..."}]` (marker frames / fps, plus the bed's start time) and `"role": "trailer"` for the bass-share note. Cut the picture to black on `visual_cut_frame`; the hit lands one frame later.
- **Measured** (`qa.py`, on the -16 LUFS-I preview master): the wall is -15.0 dBFS and the gap -53.1 dBFS (**38.1 dB under the wall**; QA needs 30); the crescendo rises **19.2 dB** into the hit (QA needs 12); the hold is **56.2 dB under the hit** (QA needs 40); the last 0.15 s is digital zero: True; every marker is a whole frame.
- **ears, mixed through `mixer.py` to -14 LUFS** (14 Oct 2026, `ears/listen.py master.wav --plan plan.json`): -14.03 LUFS-I, true peak -1.5 dBTP, AAC probes -1.46 and -1.42 dBTP. With the gap and the hold marked, `ears/structure` passes: it finds the valley at 24.0-27.0 s and the crescendo (the biggest step) at 27.0 s, which are the marker times, and reports the ending as a quiet hold; the unmarked file warns of dead air at 24.0-27.0 s and 32.5-35.5 s. Bass share (< 150 Hz) is 58%, inside the 50-61% range for a trailer bed. **One warning stays:** the loudness range is 15.9 LU over the whole file and 13.5 LU without the two planned regions (house max 10 LU), because the act-1 drone sits about 16 LU under the wall by design. Lift act 1 if a given cut needs a flatter range.
- **No human has listened to it.** The voices are the pack's owned synthesis and the arrangement is written by rule; the numbers prove the shape, not the taste. Audition it once before a hero use.

## Honest limits
- **No listening pass yet**: the kit is verified by measurement (loudness, true peak, determinism, seams, grid, chroma) and a timeline still, not by ear. The composer makes competent generic beds, not finished songs; treat them as pro-grade scratch / library beds, and expect to retune a preset after one listen.
- (closed 2026-10-13: tempo ramps exist as `anthem_ramp`; the loop bed `anthem_build` stays constant-tempo by design.) Earlier note: `anthem_build` does not accelerate (research suggested 100 -> 128 BPM); a tempo ramp needs a tempo map the grid does not support yet. `cinematic_drone` and `suspense_tick` are sparse by design.
- Indian presets are stylised owned synthesis in just intonation, not recordings or imitations; JI and the tabla/sitar models are textbook starting points (Raman 1920 for the dayan modes, a heuristic bayan glide) that need one listening pass with a musician. In-scale chroma warnings on bhangra/festive/desi beds come from untuned drum partials, not wrong notes.
- **The trailer arc has not been auditioned by a human** (see its section); its loudness range is wide by design, and the Lyria path in `cloud/` remains the only way to get a sung or orchestrally real trailer, without markers.
- The demo voice is a word-free formant babble stand-in used only to prove ducking and cue-over-voice levels. It is not speech.
- Loudness targets for Instagram / TikTok / X / LinkedIn remain unverified (D-grade); the house master is -14 LUFS-I, TP <= -1.5 on the WAV, AAC probes <= -1.0 dBTP (so HyperFrames never lowers the track).
